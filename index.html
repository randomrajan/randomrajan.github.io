01 / About

From raw sources to trusted reporting.

I'm a data analyst who works on the whole path, from pulling and cleaning messy multi-source data to designing the dashboards that leaders use to make decisions.

My recent work is in healthcare and Indigenous community programs. That includes Canadian hospital data such as DAD and NACRS, where accuracy, data quality and privacy really matter. Before that, I spent two years at Capgemini building self-serve Power BI and SAP BI reporting for operations, sales and finance teams.

I work across the whole stack: the pipelines that move and validate the data (Azure Data Factory, and I'm a certified Databricks Data Engineer Associate), the models behind the numbers, and the dashboards people actually use.

I like turning vague questions into clear KPI definitions, then delivering reports that are reliable, well documented and easy for non-technical users to understand.
